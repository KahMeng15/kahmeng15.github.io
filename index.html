Smart Rainwater Harvesting Tank System (SMARHATS)

SMARHATS is an IoT-based system idea designed to improve water conservation and quality monitoring for Malaysian households. It uses sensors to track water levels, pH, and temperature, automates drainage, and provides real-time alerts via a mobile app. The project, simulated in Packet Tracer, demonstrates efficient, sustainable water management, though challenges include initial costs and technical complexity.

A networking project created by myself, Qaiyyum and Janarrtha Rao

As the Head of Secretariat Affairs, I was responsible for overseeing the planning and execution of a wide range of college events and activities. My role involved coordinating and supporting program directors from the initial concept to the final wrap-up, ensuring that every program ran smoothly and met its objectives. I empower program directors to lead their teams effectively, providing guidance, resources, and encouragement whenever needed.

During my tenure, I noticed a lack of clear guidelines for internal documentation, which often led to confusion and inefficiencies. My first major initiative was to create templates and guideline documents to standardize the design and documentation process. This not only streamlined workflows but also made it easier for students to organize events efficiently and maintain consistency across programs. By improving these processes, I helped foster a culture of organization and accountability within the college community. I also worked closely with other council members to ensure that every event was inclusive, well-documented, and provided opportunities for personal growth and leadership development.

I worked closely with my deputy secretariat members, Stephanie Ngu and Eddy, to manage the college's digital presence and official event organization. Throughout our tenure, we established a stronger digital presence online, enhancing the college's visibility and engagement with students and the wider community. We successfully organized various events that promoted digital multimedia knowledge, providing additional skills and opportunities for students to grow.

One of our proudest achievements was creating the first college magazine, which documented college events and activities for future generations. We also took responsibility for programs with official ceremonies, ensuring that protocols, scripts, seating arrangements, and technical aspects were handled professionally. By focusing on both the creative and logistical sides of event management, we helped elevate the college's reputation and fostered a sense of pride among students and staff. Our work not only improved communication and documentation but also inspired others to take initiative and contribute to the college's ongoing success.
